Social Impact Assessment (SIA) Public Review

Important: This is a voluntary, community-led public review process. Participation is open and non-mandatory. The SIAP framework and any future SIAP registration processes are currently under development and are not compulsory. This process is intended for South African stakeholders, as it aligns with the National Environmental Management Act (NEMA) and South African environmental assessment practice.

This page hosts a draft Social Impact Assessment Practitioner (SIAP) Professional Framework for public comment. The purpose is to invite feedback from practitioners, communities, academics, regulators, and other stakeholders involved in Social Impact Assessment in South Africa.
